# Portfolio Case — NIS2 Incident Reporting Timeline

## Problem
During a significant cyber incident, teams need to track notification milestones, evidence, ownership and decisions while facts are still incomplete.

## Operational timeline
```text
Awareness
   ↓
24h Early Warning
   ↓
72h Incident Notification
   ↓
Intermediate updates when applicable
   ↓
One-month Final Report
   ↓
Evidence-ready decision record
```

## What this demonstrates
- Regulatory deadlines translated into operational workflow
- Live deadline tracking
- Evidence-completeness checks
- Approval and submission states
- Decision logging
- EN/ES delivery
- Local-first privacy design

## Product direction
Use the free timeline as an incident-readiness diagnostic and connect mature use cases to incident governance, notification playbooks and professional readiness support.

## Professional boundary
The tool does not submit regulatory notifications or replace applicable national reporting requirements and legal analysis.
